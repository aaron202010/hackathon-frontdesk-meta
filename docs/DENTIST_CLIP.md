# The dentist clip (20 to 30 seconds, opens the demo video)

Who: one practising dentist in the Philippines, on camera, in their own clinic or at home. Their own words; the lines below are a guide, not a script to read. Permission in writing (a reply "yes, you can use it in the hackathon video and the public repo" is enough), kept with the raw file.

## What we ask them to say (any order, Taglish or English, their way)
1. The problem: "Patients message our page at night or on weekends asking if we take their HMO. Nobody can answer until the morning, and by then some have booked elsewhere."
2. The HMO part: "Half the questions are 'covered ba ako?'. We have to check the card and the list by hand every time."
3. What they want: "If someone could read the card, tell them what our clinic accepts, and book them, and leave the medical questions to us, that would save the front desk hours."

## Do not ask them to say
- Anything about HeyDenta or this product being good. They describe the problem, not us.
- Numbers they do not know first hand (lost patients, revenue).
- Any patient's name or detail.

## How to film
- Phone, vertical is fine, face and shoulders, quiet room, window light in front of them.
- Three takes, 20 to 40 seconds each. We pick one and cut to 20 to 30.
- Say their name and clinic city at the start only if they agree to be named; otherwise "a dentist in Quezon City" on a caption.

## Where it goes
The first 20 to 30 seconds of the 3 minute video, then the HMO card demo. One line in the write-up: "the problem, in a practising dentist's words". The raw file stays out of the repo.
